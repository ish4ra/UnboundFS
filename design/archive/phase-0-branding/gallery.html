UnboundFS · Phase 0 · Brand identity review

Eight directions for a filesystem utility that crosses platforms.

Selected by the project owner: Concept 05 — Shear (Silver & Ember). This page is now an archive of the Phase 0 exploration. The production identity lives in design/brand/; the other seven concepts are kept for reference only.

Each concept has an app icon, a standalone symbol, light, dark and monochrome versions, a menu bar template, a wordmark and its own palette.

Concept 01

Beyond

Graphite & Mint

The nine-dot puzzle: join all nine points with four straight lines without lifting the pen. It can only be solved by drawing past the edge of the box. Nine blocks of storage, one continuous mint line through every one of them, and two corners that sit deliberately outside the boundary.

Concept 02

Unfolded

Midnight & Cyan

A cube is the usual picture of a volume. Here it is laid open as its six-face net, with the last face still folding flat. A sealed container turns into a surface you can see and use: the drive opened up, no matter how its box was built.

Concept 04

Keystone

Basalt & Copper

A U assembled the way stone bridges are: separate voussoir blocks locked in place by a keystone. The blocks are storage, the arch is the bridge between platforms, and the copper keystone is the piece UnboundFS supplies, the one that makes the whole structure hold. A lettermark that carries a story about integrity.

Concept 06

Loose Leaf

Ink Violet & Vellum

'Unbound' is a bookbinding word: pages that have left the spine. Three dog-eared leaves fan out from one pivot, files released from the format that held them while still sharing a single origin. Quiet, literate, and tied directly to the name.

Concept 07

Pathfinder

Forest & Saffron

A squared spiral is a maze path, and a filesystem is a maze of paths. The line starts at a saffron core, winds through every layer and leaves the structure without being stopped. A wayfinding mark: from a closed format to an open route.

Concept 08

Interlink

Lagoon & Coral

A lowercase u and n, the same letterform turned half a revolution, linked like chain links with real over-and-under crossings. It spells the 'un' in UnboundFS and shows two equal systems holding on to each other: two colours for two platforms, one connection.

Reviewer shortlist at the time (advisory)

  1. Concept 04 · Keystone

    Most ownable. A true lettermark with a story about structural integrity, crisp at every size, and the warm light icon stands apart from the usual dark utility icons.

  2. Concept 08 · Interlink

    The cleverest idea in the set: u and n, the same glyph rotated, physically linked. Distinctive silhouette, strong two-colour system, works in one colour.

  3. Concept 01 · Beyond

    The most precise expression of the name: the nine-dot puzzle is solved only by leaving the box. Calm, technical and very recognisable once seen.

This was a suggestion only. The owner selected Concept 05 Shear.
